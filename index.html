Top three places to visit

Motherland Monument

The Motherland Monument is one of Kyiv's most famous landmarks. Enjoy the impressive statue and beautiful views of the city.

Explore the Golden Gate

The Golden Gate is a historic landmark in the heart of Kyiv. Learn about the city's history and enjoy this beautiful monument.

Visit St. Sophia's Cathedral

St. Sophia's Cathedral is one of Kyiv's most famous historic places. Admire its beautiful architecture and golden domes.
